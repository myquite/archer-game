# CRYPTBOW — 16-Bit Sprite-Stacked Dungeon ARPG

A vertical portrait-oriented medieval dungeon archer roguelike built with HTML5 Canvas **sprite stacking** (fake-3D layered pixel extrusion), dynamic additive magical lighting, synergy evolutions, boss relics, and iPhone-friendly one-thumb touch controls.

## Play Online
Hosted on GitHub Pages: **https://myquite.github.io/archer-game/**

*(On iPhone Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for native fullscreen standalone play.)*

## Features
- **True Sprite-Stacking Engine:** Vertical pixel slices per character, boss, and prop with automatic dark 1px outlines, 360° fake-3D rotation, live animated bowstring/arrow rig, velocity layer lag, and destructible dungeon props.
- **Move to Charge, Stop to Snap:** Drag anywhere (or `WASD`/Arrows) to charge momentum; lift/pause to unleash a heavy piercing **Snap Shot**, dodge anti-camp mortars, and flank shielded Hellguards.
- **5 Chapters & 8 Handcrafted Arenas:** Torchlit Crypts, Plague Catacombs, Molten Forge, Frost Sanctum, and Abyssal Vault.
- **8 Weapons & 8 Visible Armor Layer Sets:** Swapping gear at the **Master Blacksmith** physically swaps your archer's stacked 3D pixel layers and live bow rig.
- **5 Synergy Evolutions & 6 Boss Relics:** Awaken *Comet Lance, Shatterstorm, Tempest Barrage, Blood Eclipse,* and *Phantom Volley*, pop sprite-stacked **Ironbound Relic Chests**, and spend **Reroll Dice**.
- **16-Bit Dynamic Soundtrack & SFX:** Multi-section Web Audio chiptune themes per chapter, boss rush music, balanced SFX throttling, and unified 16×16 pixel-art UI icons.
