# ARCHER — 16-Bit Sprite-Stacked Dungeon ARPG

A vertical portrait-oriented medieval dungeon archer shooter built with HTML5 Canvas **sprite stacking** (fake-3D layered pixel extrusion), dynamic additive magical lighting, synergy evolutions, boss relics, and iPhone-friendly one-thumb touch controls.

## Play Online
Hosted on GitHub Pages: **https://myquite.github.io/archer-game/**

*(On iPhone Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** for native fullscreen standalone play.)*

## Features
- **True Sprite-Stacking Engine:** 10–21 vertical pixel slices per character, boss, and prop with automatic dark 1px outlines, 360° fake-3D rotation, velocity layer lag, bow recoil accordion, peeling enemy armor layers, and chipping stone pillars.
- **Kite & Snap Combat:** Drag anywhere (or `WASD`/Arrows) to kite & auto-fire; lift/pause to charge a heavy piercing **Snap Shot**.
- **Interactive Dungeon:** Shoot arrows through flaming braziers to ignite them into Fire Arrows; detonate oil barrels; take cover behind destructible pillars.
- **5 Weapons & 4 Visible Armor Layer Sets:** Swapping gear at the **Master Blacksmith** physically swaps your archer's stacked 3D pixel layers.
- **5 Synergy Evolutions & 6 Boss Relics:** Awaken *Comet Lance, Shatterstorm, Tempest Barrage, Blood Eclipse,* and *Phantom Volley*, pop glowing sprite-stacked **Ironbound Relic Chests**, and spend **Reroll Dice**.
- **Dynamic Magical Lighting & 16-Bit Web Audio SFX:** Live additive light pools under every arrow, spell, brazier, and wall torch, plus zero-asset synthesized retro sound effects.
